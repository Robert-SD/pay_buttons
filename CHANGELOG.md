## 0.1.0

* Initial release of `pay_buttons`.
* Included cross-platform payment buttons:
  * Apple Pay (native `PKPaymentButton` on iOS and Web JS SDK)
  * Google Pay (native Android via `package:pay` and Web JS SDK)
  * PayPal & PayPal Pay Later
  * Klarna
  * Shop Pay
  * Afterpay / Clearpay
  * Wero
  * iDEAL | Wero
  * BLIK
  * TWINT
  * Bancontact
  * Bizum
  * Pix
  * Boleto Bancário
  * OXXO
  * Alipay
  * WeChat Pay
  * PayNow
  * PromptPay
  * UPI
* Responsive layouts, custom fonts, typography fallback support, and full accessibility semantics.
